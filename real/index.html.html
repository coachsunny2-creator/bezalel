Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you navigate property transactions in Nigeria.

How do I view a property?

Click "Book an Inspection" on any listing or reach us directly on WhatsApp. We schedule physical tours Mondays through Saturdays at your convenience.

Can I buy property from another city or abroad?

Yes. Many of our clients reside outside Nigeria. We provide high-definition live video walkthroughs, digital dossiers, and courier original deeds upon legal execution.

Can I pay in instalments?

Selected properties and our direct developments (such as Royal Gardens) offer milestone-based payment schedules. Individual resale properties are subject to vendor terms.

What documents should I check before buying land?

Key documents include the Certificate of Occupancy (C of O), Governor's Consent, Registered Survey Plan, and Deed of Assignment. We recommend independent legal verification of every title.

Where does Bezalel Royal Properties operate?

Our active core portfolio focuses on Abuja (FCT), Lagos State, and Port Harcourt (Rivers State).
